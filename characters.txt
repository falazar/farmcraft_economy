Faewild:
Faewild is a distant city to the south, it has pigs and a mystical forest nearby.
Exports include apple, cherry, cinnamon and maple from their large tree nurseries and dark oak wood. 
They had just started a soybean industry, but a blight has devastated the crops and most soybeans will not grow here now.
Faewild is renowned for its grand mystical church that stands majestically in the center of town.
Beneath the city lie extensive lush caves where a thriving clay and terracotta mining operation works tirelessly.
A well-maintained train line connects the city to the mysterious south lands.
Recently, explorers have discovered a deep dark biome nearby, and an investigation team has been assembled to explore its depths.

34. Elira Moonwhisper
Rae: Elf
Role: Druid and guardian of the Mystic Grove

Description: Elira is a calm, enigmatic presence in the village of Faewild. She speaks in soft riddles and tends to the magical flora of the nearby grove. Her bond with nature runs deep, and she is trusted by both fairy folk and villagers alike.


4. Borin Stoneguard
Race: Dwarf
Role: Mine overseer and protector

Description: A stout, practical dwarf with years of experience in the mines beneath Pymosia. Borin values safety, discipline, and a good mug of mushroom ale. He's quick to raise the alarm when danger threatens his people.


5. Captain Thorne Blackwood
Role: Commander of the Faewild Border Guard and Brennick's rival

Description: Captain Blackwood is a stern but fair military leader who has been defending Faewild's borders against Lord Brennick's encroachments. A former royal guard, he brings discipline and tactical expertise to the conflict. He believes strongly in Faewild's right to the disputed lands and has become Brennick's primary adversary in the ongoing territorial dispute.
