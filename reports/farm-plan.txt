PRACTICAL FIRST TRIP

Together, those raise the earlier home baseline from 57 to 80 crop types.
Start with Tundra, then Old Growth Spruce Taiga.

HOME-FARM OPPORTUNITY

The earlier analysis found one farmland block in Shrubland. Including it,
your city technically supports 57 crop types, not the initial estimate of 45.
That tiny patch adds lettuce, blueberry, parsnip, kohlrabi, waterchestnut,
and other crops, but sharing one block is impractical.

Village level 6 unlocks farm plot level 3. Upgrade the relevant farm chunk
to level 3 to use crop permissions from any biome present in that chunk
at planting height, throughout that chunk. This is not village-wide.
Below farm level 3, the exact planting block's biome still matters.

WHERE BOG FITS

COVERAGE LIMITS
